# Screen-off conversations — phone test sheet (0.5.0)

The emulator checked the parts it can: listening carries on with the screen off, the notification and its
buttons, the earphone button (sent the way Bluetooth earbuds send it), the pocket sensor, and End releasing
everything. These need a real phone, ideally two makes (for example a Samsung and a Xiaomi or Pixel).

Before you start: Settings → Conversation → **Keep listening with the screen off** and **Reply with your earphone
button** are on (they are by default).

## 1. First conversation
- [ ] Tap Listen. Android asks for the microphone, then (Android 13+) to show notifications. Allow both.
- [ ] A notification "Anuvaad · Listening to …" appears with **Speak · Pause · End**.

## 2. Screen off and other apps
- [ ] Lock the phone. Have the other person speak: their translation plays in your earphones.
- [ ] Open another app (WhatsApp, Maps): the conversation keeps going; the green microphone dot shows.
- [ ] After a minute of silence it pauses; the notification says **Paused** with **Listen · Speak · End**.

## 3. Lock-screen buttons
- [ ] Press the power button: the notification is on the lock screen. **Speak** works without unlocking.
- [ ] While you speak it shows **Done · End**; Done sends your reply, which plays on the phone speaker.
- [ ] With Settings → **Review my reply** on: the notification says "Your reply is ready" with **Play · Discard**.
- [ ] **End** removes the notification and the green microphone dot.

## 4. Earphone button
- [ ] Bluetooth earbuds: one tap (play/pause) starts your reply, a second tap sends it.
- [ ] Wired earphones with a button (if you have them): the same.
- [ ] After End, the earbud tap controls music again.

## 5. Pocket
- [ ] With a conversation running and the app open, put the phone in a pocket: the screen turns off; take it out:
      it comes back on.

## 6. Reply without tapping (Android 14+, two people)
- [ ] Turn on Settings → **Reply without tapping**. Listen, then speak a full sentence in your language: it becomes
      your reply (translated and played on the phone speaker) without tapping Speak.
- [ ] The other person speaks a sentence with one or two English words in it: it stays their turn.
