# Changelog

Release notes for Anuvaad. Each release needs an entry in exactly this format (see docs/release-process.md):
the heading `## X.Y.Z (BUILD) — YYYY-MM-DD`, optional `Mandatory: yes|no` and `Min supported build: N` lines,
then `### What's new` and/or `### Fixed` with `- ` bullets. The app shows these notes in its update popup.

## 0.5.0 (5) — 2026-11-13
Mandatory: no
Min supported build: 1
### What's new
- Conversations with the screen off: lock the phone or switch apps and the conversation keeps going, with a notification while it runs.
- Reply from the lock screen with the notification's Speak button, or press play/pause on your earphones.
- Reply without tapping (Android 14 and later, off by default): when Anuvaad hears your language, it translates it for them.
- In a pocket the screen turns off by itself, as it does during a phone call.

## 0.4.0 (4) — 2026-11-13
Mandatory: no
Min supported build: 1
### What's new
- Hands-free conversations: Listen keeps listening and takes turns by itself, with Pause and End.
- Automatic language detection on Android 14 and later, which asks when it isn't sure.
- Check and edit your reply before it plays (optional).
- Save conversations on your phone (off by default), then search, copy, share or delete them.
- Choose a voice and speech speed, see each language's real status, and use the app in Hindi or Gujarati.
- Meeting mode for meetings played on a speaker, and a latency report.

## 0.3.0 (3) — 2026-11-06
Mandatory: no
Min supported build: 1
### What's new
- Translations are spoken: the other person's words in your earphones, your reply aloud on the phone speaker.
- Replay any translation, and see which device played it.
- Audio devices screen: choose where each voice plays and test the sound.

## 0.2.0 (2) — 2026-11-06
Mandatory: no
Min supported build: 1
### What's new
- Conversation preview: tap Listen or Speak, see live text as you talk, then its translation between English, Hindi and Gujarati.
- Offline translation after a one-time download on Wi-Fi, clearly labelled.

## 0.1.0 (1) — 2026-11-05
Mandatory: no
Min supported build: 1
### What's new
- First test build: onboarding, language selection, light and dark themes, and automatic updates.
